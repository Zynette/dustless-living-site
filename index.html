About Dustless Living

Dustless Living began as a solo passion project by Antonette Petallo, balancing tech, school, and a commitment to creating calming, spotless spaces for busy people in Hamilton.

What started as a personal cleaning service has grown into a trusted, detail-obsessed local brand serving homes, rentals, and businesses across Hamilton & Burlington — with the same care, consistency, and respect as day one.

Our mission is simple: a clean space, a clear mind. We believe in transparent pricing, respectful communication, and leaving every client feeling lighter the moment we step out the door.
