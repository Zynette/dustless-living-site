A standard clean is ideal for homes that are lived-in but not extremely messy. It focuses on:

  • Dusting reachable surfaces and decor
  • Wiping counters, tables, and visible fingerprints
  • Bathroom refresh (toilet, sink, tub/shower, mirrors)
  • Kitchen refresh (stovetop wipe, exterior of appliances, sink)
  • Spot vacuuming/sweeping & mopping main floors using your vacuum & mop
  • Light tidying such as straightening pillows and blankets

It’s meant to keep things maintained between deeper resets – perfect for recurring weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly visits.

Deep cleans go further into the “hidden” areas that collect dust, grease, and buildup. In addition to everything in a standard clean, a deep clean may include:

  • Baseboards, door frames, and switch plates
  • Inside microwave and small appliances
  • More detailed bathroom scrubbing and descaling
  • Extra time for tough areas like shower grout, stove knobs, and backsplashes
  • Extra tidying in high-traffic spaces (entryway, living room, kitchen)

For most first-time visits, we recommend a deep clean first, then lighter recurring maintenance afterwards.

Not always, but often. The first visit is where we “reset” your space so it’s easier (and cheaper) to maintain afterward.

If your home is already very tidy and just needs light upkeep, we may start with a standard clean. If there is visible buildup, clutter, or it has been a long time since your last professional clean, a deep clean or move-in/move-out style visit is usually recommended.

To keep everyone safe and realistic, some tasks are outside our normal scope or require a custom quote:

  • Heavy decluttering, hoarding situations, or large trash removal
  • Biohazards (animal waste, infestations, strong nicotine, mold, bodily fluids)
  • Large furniture moving, appliance disconnection, or handyman-style repairs
  • Exterior windows, high ladder work, or outdoor power washing

If you’re unsure, send photos or mention it in your booking notes and we’ll let you know if it’s something we can do or refer out.

Pricing is a mix of home size, mess level, add-ons, and travel. During quoting, we ask you to rate your home from 1–10:

  • 1–3 represents an already tidy space that just needs a reset.
  • 4–6 reflects an average lived-in home.
  • 7–8 signals heavier mess with buildup or clutter.
  • 9–10 indicates a very challenging clean that requires extra time.

This helps us estimate realistic hours and energy. Final pricing is always confirmed with you before we lock anything in.

No need to “pre-clean” for your cleaner. But a few small steps help us spend more time on actual cleaning:

  • Do basic tidying of dishes where possible
  • Clear floors of toys, clothes, and personal belongings as much as you can
  • Put away important documents, cash, and valuables so nothing is accidentally moved
  • Make sure we know about any alarms, pets, or parking instructions

If life is chaotic and you can’t tidy beforehand, just let us know and we’ll adjust expectations and the quote together.

Cleaning supplies, microfiber system, and steam equipment are always included. For most visits, we can use your vacuum to keep things consistent for your floors and to avoid cross-home equipment issues. If you don’t have one, let us know — we can bring an option when needed.

We lean toward low-tox, pet-conscious, and kid-friendly formulas and avoid using harsh products unless absolutely necessary for heavy buildup.

If someone in your home has asthma, allergies, or is sensitive to fragrance, please tell us in advance. We can:

  • Ventilate more carefully while we work
