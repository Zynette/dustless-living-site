Why Dustless Living feels different

Owner-led, steam-first, low-tox cleaning for calm, respected spaces.

Dustless Living began as a one-woman service by Antonette, a detail-oriented cleaner with a background in tech support and a soft spot for calm, organized spaces. It’s still personal today, and I’m growing slowly and carefully.

I support homes, landlords, students, busy families, and small businesses across Hamilton & Burlington with careful, respectful work and a clean space that feels safe, cared for, and unrushed.
