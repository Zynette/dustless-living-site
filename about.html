About Dustless Living

Dustless Living began as a one-woman service by Antonette, a detail-oriented cleaner with a background in tech support and a soft spot for calm, organized spaces. It’s still personal today, with a second set of hands in training to join for busier weeks.

Today, Dustless Living supports homes, landlords, students, busy families, and small businesses across Hamilton & Burlington — thoughtfully, respectfully, and without shortcuts.

Our promise is simple: a clean space that feels safe, respected, and cared for. No rushed “in-and-out” jobs, no hidden fees. Just reliable people doing careful work.

Cleaning-first focus

Every decision revolves around healthier homes.

From the supplies we mix to the rotations we schedule, Dustless Living is run like a boutique cleaning studio — not a gig marketplace. You always know who’s walking in the door and which checklist they’re following.

Personal routines

We keep notes on pet names, crib nap times, and appliance finishes so every visit feels like the same respectful neighbour stopping by.

Steam & low-tox

Pro-grade steam tools step in for grout, tile, toys, and soft goods when chemicals aren’t welcome. Otherwise, we rely on plant-based concentrates and refills.

Calm communication

You get confirmations, arrival texts, and a short aftercare recap with photos so you’re never guessing what was completed.

Supplies & standards

We arrive self-contained.

You never have to stock specialty products. I restock the DL Clean Kit before every visit, sanitize all tools between homes, and use your vacuum/mop so we don’t carry germs across households.

  • ✔Color-coded microfiber sets & detail brushes
  • ✔Glass, wood, and stone-safe blends
  • ✔Dry-steam wand for sanitizing
  • ✔Dedicated pet-hair attachments
  • ✔Your vacuum & mop stay on-site to avoid cross contamination

Visit rhythm

  • Arrival walkthrough & priorities
  • Declutter, dust, detail surfaces
  • Kitchen + bath deep focus
  • Floors vacuumed / mopped using your equipment
  • Steam add-ons if booked
  • Final styling + recap text
